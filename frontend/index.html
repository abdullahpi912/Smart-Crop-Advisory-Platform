How It Works

Three simple steps stand between your field data and a confident crop decision.

1. Enter Field Data

Soil nutrients (N, P, K), temperature, humidity, pH, and rainfall.

2. Model Analyzes It

A trained ML model matches your conditions against crop-yield patterns.

3. Get Your Recommendation

See the crop best suited to your land, with confidence you can act on.

About AgriSense

AgriSense is a full-stack crop advisory platform built as part of the Innolift Ventures Full Stack AI Developer Program. It pairs a machine learning model trained on soil and climate data with a simple web interface so farmers can get a crop recommendation in seconds.

This page is the initial frontend implementation — the interface is in place, and the ML model and backend will be connected in a later phase of the build.
