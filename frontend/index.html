Soil & Advisory FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how key soil parameters affect crop matching and soil vitality.

Why are NPK values critical for crop selection?
Nitrogen (N) fuels vegetative leaf development, Phosphorus (P) accelerates root vigor and flowering, while Potassium (K) improves drought resilience and disease immunity. Balanced NPK levels ensure crops thrive without excessive chemical fertilizer application.
How does soil pH impact nutrient uptake?
Soil pH governs nutrient solubility. Strongly acidic (pH < 5.5) or alkaline (pH > 7.5) soils lock nutrients in chemical bonds, making them inaccessible to plant roots even if present in the ground. AgriSense factors pH directly into crop recommendations.
Can I test preset soil profiles before entering my own field numbers?
Yes! Visit the Get Recommendation page to try instant pure-CSS presets (such as Wetland Rice, Highland Coffee, Semi-Arid Cotton, or Arid Chickpea).

Soil & Climate Parameter Specs

Here is how AgriSense processes each of the 7 core agricultural field indicators:

  • Nitrogen (N): Target range 0–140 kg/ha. Essential for dark green foliage.
  • Phosphorus (P): Target range 5–145 kg/ha. Stimulates seed germination and rooting.
  • Potassium (K): Target range 5–205 kg/ha. Enhances cell wall rigidity and fruit quality.
  • Temperature (°C): Optimal range 8–45 °C. Governs enzyme activation.
  • Humidity (%): Target range 14–100%. Regulates plant transpiration rates.
  • Soil pH: Optimal range 3.5–9.9. Standard neutral pH is ~6.5.
  • Rainfall (mm): Seasonal precipitation from 20mm to 300mm+.
