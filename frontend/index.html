Soil & Advisory FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how key parameters affect crop matching and soil health.

Why are NPK values critical for crop selection?
Nitrogen (N) supports leaf development, Phosphorus (P) promotes root vigor and flowering, while Potassium (K) helps drought resistance and disease immunity. Balanced NPK levels determine which crops will thrive without excessive chemical fertilizers.
How does soil pH impact nutrient uptake?
Soil pH governs the chemical form of nutrients. Highly acidic (pH < 5.5) or alkaline (pH > 7.5) soils can lock nutrients, making them unavailable even if present in the soil. AgriSense takes pH into account to suggest suitable crops.
Can I test preset soil profiles before entering my own readings?
Yes! Head to the Get Recommendation page to try instant preset soil profiles (like Wetland Paddy, Highland Coffee, or Semi-Arid Cotton).
